Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Spendora ("we", "us") is a personal finance and budgeting app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendora collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By default, Spendora stores all of your data locally on your device. If you choose to create an account, your data is also stored in the cloud so you can back it up and sync it across devices.

Information We Collect

• Account information: if you create an account, we collect your display name and email address to authenticate you, via Firebase Authentication. You can change your display name at any time from your Profile. • Financial data you enter: transactions, wallets, categories, budgets, and goals you add to the app, including amounts, dates, notes, colors, and labels you choose to enter. • Usage data: basic information about how the app is used — such as which screens are opened and which actions are performed (for example "added a transaction" or "created a budget") — via Google Analytics for Firebase. These events never include your amounts, notes, or other financial details. If you are signed in, usage events are associated with your account's internal ID. • Diagnostic data: if the app crashes, basic crash and device diagnostic information may be collected to help us fix bugs, via Firebase Crashlytics. This data is not linked to your identity. • Advertising identifiers: Spendora shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier (with your permission) and general device information to select and measure ads. • Device settings: your device's language and region are read on-device to suggest a default language and currency. This information is not sent to us. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or camera data, and your financial data is never shared with our advertising partners.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to provide the app's core functionality (recording and displaying your transactions, budgets, and goals), authenticate your account and keep your data in sync across your devices if you sign in, back up your data to the cloud so you do not lose it if you lose your device, understand which features are used so we can improve the app, and diagnose and fix crashes and bugs. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your financial data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Notifications

Spendora can send you local notifications on your device, such as budget alerts, goal milestones, and reminders for recurring transactions. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device — they are not sent from our servers and do not involve any additional data collection. Your device will ask for your permission before the first notification is shown, and you can change this at any time from your device's Settings.

Where Your Data Is Stored

By default, all data you enter stays only on your device, in a local database. If you sign in with an email and password, your data is additionally stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google, and kept in sync between your devices. Signing out removes your data from that device but keeps it in your cloud account, so you can sign back in and restore it; changes that have not yet been synced are lost when you sign out, and the app warns you before this happens. Deleting your account removes your data from our cloud servers as described below.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We use Google Firebase as our cloud infrastructure provider for authentication, cloud storage (Firestore), usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), and crash reporting (Crashlytics), and Google AdMob to show ads. These providers process data on our behalf under Google's own privacy and security terms. We do not share your financial data with any other third party, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

Advertising

Spendora shows ads through Google AdMob to support the app. If you are in a region covered by GDPR (EEA, UK, or Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. On iOS, we also ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, you'll see non-personalized ads instead, and you can still use every feature of the app. You can change your choice at any time from your device's Settings. Ad networks never receive your transactions, budgets, or goals.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you use Spendora and keep your account. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account Management → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data — transactions, wallets, budgets, and goals — from both your device and our cloud servers, and cannot be undone. Removing the app from your device deletes locally stored data. Usage and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google's standard retention settings.

Security

Spendora supports app-level protection via a device PIN or Face ID / Touch ID, through the platform's secure biometric frameworks; your PIN is stored securely on-device using Secure Store. Data sent to our cloud is encrypted in transit, and Firebase secures data at rest on Google's infrastructure. Backup files you export are not encrypted, so please store them somewhere safe.

Your Choices & Rights

You can use Spendora as a guest without ever creating an account, in which case your financial data never leaves your device. You can export a backup file of your local data at any time from Settings → Backup & data, and import it later to restore. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and data at any time, either in-app or by contacting us below.

Children's Privacy

Spendora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at hoanlk.dev@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
